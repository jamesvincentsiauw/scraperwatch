# Evaluation

Generated by `python -m simulation.simulate --seeds 30` with scraperwatch 0.1.0.dev0.

Each scenario runs 30 simulated days for each of 30 seeds (0 to 29). The first 10 days are healthy, then one incident lasts 1 to 3 days at a random point. Healthy days include normal noise: listings added and removed, missing ratings, optional discounts, and category sizes that change from day to day. The scraper is configured with `required=["title", "price"]` and `key=["id"]`.

Detected means one of the expected checks fired on an incident day, at any severity. Quarantined means the run's verdict was quarantine. A false alarm is a warn or quarantine verdict on a healthy day, including the first days while the baseline warms up.

Overall: 384/384 incident days detected (100%), 0 false alarms in 5016 healthy days (0.00 per 30 runs).

Scenarios 3 and 4 have dedicated checks planned for 0.2 (template fingerprints and block detection). In 0.1 they are caught through their effect: pages that yield no items.

Scenario 6 is detected as a warning, by design: unique items fall by about a quarter, which is under the volume threshold that would make it a quarantine.

These numbers hold for the fake shop's noise levels: up to 5% of listings replaced and 5% added per category per day, 1% of prices and 3% of ratings missing. Real sites can be noisier, so a clean result here does not guarantee zero false alarms elsewhere.
